01 · PROFILE

Safety grounded in operations.

I understand industrial safety because I have worked inside industrial operations.

My path started with a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, followed by training in industrial automation across DCS, PLC and SCADA. That grounding led to a control-room role at Ma’aden Gold's 63 MW captive power plant in Saudi Arabia, where I operated six Wärtsilä engine units through DCS/SCADA.

Safety was part of the daily job: PTW issuance and monitoring, LOTO isolations, field coordination, abnormal operating conditions, hot work, confined space and electrical isolation.

In January 2026 I moved to Tasnee as Scheduler on the LDPE & HDPE turnaround, adding shutdown planning, contractor interfaces, progress control and Primavera P6 experience to my operational base. I am now targeting dedicated HSE roles while completing NEBOSH IGC.
