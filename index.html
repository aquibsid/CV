01 · PROFILE

Safety grounded in operations.

You get a safety professional who understands the operating environment behind the permit.

My path started with a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, followed by industrial automation training across DCS, PLC and SCADA.

That foundation led to a control-room role at Ma’aden Gold's 63 MW captive power plant in Saudi Arabia, where I operated six Wärtsilä engine units through DCS/SCADA.

Safety was part of the daily job: PTW issuance and monitoring, LOTO isolations, field coordination, abnormal operating conditions, hot work, confined space and electrical isolation.

In January 2026 I moved to Tasnee as Scheduler on the LDPE & HDPE turnaround, adding shutdown planning, contractor interfaces, progress control and Primavera P6 experience.
